# HEAVI ENERGY

## Solar Site Screening for Renewable Development

---

### What it does

Score candidate parcels against **15 federal and open data sources** with documented,
peer-reviewed methodology, **confidence scoring**, and **interconnection context** — so you
know both the answer and how much to trust it.

### How it works

Upload parcels (CSV or enter addresses) → automated scoring against **14 criteria** (9
scored, 5 exclusion) → **ranked results on a map** → **PDF export** with full methodology
documentation. Geocoding, batch portfolios, and per-parcel assessments included.

### What makes it different

Every assessment tells you the **score AND how much to trust it** — which data sources were
used, where the gaps are, and the peer-reviewed methodology backing each criterion. It is
**deterministic**: identical inputs always produce identical outputs, so every assessment is
auditable and reproducible — unlike AI answer engines.

### Validated

- **71% of real EIA solar installations score High** across 10 US states (greenfield-eligible).
- **Positive discrimination in all 10 of 10 states** — real installations outscore matched
  random rural land everywhere.
- Weights **calibrated per NERC region against 6,321 EIA Form 860 installations**, producing
  seven region-specific profiles.
- Interconnection context from **LBNL "Queued Up" 2025** (4,426 active solar projects).

*Full methodology and validation are published in the Heavi Solar Methodology Whitepaper.*
