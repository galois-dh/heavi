# Heavi Energy — Design Partner Pilot Agreement

**Pilot Duration:** 90 days  
**Pilot Cost:** Free  
**Parties:** Heavi Energy and ____________________ ("Design Partner")

---

## Purpose

A time-boxed, no-cost pilot to evaluate whether Heavi's solar site-suitability platform
saves the Design Partner time in early-stage parcel screening, and to measure the platform's
**precision** against the Partner's own expert judgment. The pilot is structured so both
sides know, at day 90, whether continued use is worth a paid subscription.

---

## What Heavi Provides

- **Scoring of up to 200 candidate parcels** through the solar suitability platform, against
  14 criteria drawn from 15 federal and open data sources, with per-NERC-region calibrated
  weights.
- **Per-parcel PDF assessment** with score, confidence tier, per-criterion breakdown naming
  the data source used for each criterion, and full methodology documentation.
- **Batch results** with map visualization and a ranked, color-coded list (High / Moderate /
  Low / Excluded), exportable as a portfolio PDF.
- **Interconnection context** from the LBNL "Queued Up" 2025 queue dataset: nearest
  substation, existing connected capacity within 50 km, and nearby active solar queue
  activity (informational, not a power-flow study).
- **Weekly check-in calls** to capture feedback and answer methodology questions.
- **The methodology whitepaper** as a citable reference for the Partner's internal or
  investment-committee use.

---

## What the Design Partner Provides

- **50–200 candidate parcels** currently in their screening pipeline (coordinates or
  addresses; any funnel stage).
- **Expert review of Heavi's top-scored parcels** — classifying each High-scored parcel as
  **would-pursue**, **would-not-pursue**, or **already-known**, with a brief reason for any
  "would not pursue" (see the Precision Metric Framework).
- **Feedback** on: which criteria matter most to their decisions, what is missing, and what
  would make them pay.
- **A 30-minute call at days 30, 60, and 90.**

---

## Success Criteria (defined jointly, up front)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Precision** | (would-pursue + already-known) ÷ High-scored parcels | **≥ 70%** |
| **Coverage** | Partner-selected parcels Heavi also scores High ÷ partner-selected parcels | **≥ 80%** |
| **Value** | Partner reports time savings vs. their current screening workflow | Qualitative, captured at day 90 |

Precision and coverage are defined precisely in the companion **Precision Metric Framework**.
Both are measured on the Partner's own parcels, so the results reflect the Partner's
geography and segment — not a generic benchmark.

---

## Pilot Timeline

| Day | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0 | Kickoff: Partner delivers parcel list; success criteria confirmed |
| 1–7 | Heavi scores all parcels; delivers batch results, map, and per-parcel PDFs |
| 30 | Review call: walk through results; Partner begins expert review of High-scored parcels |
| 60 | Review call: precision and coverage computed; failure reasons logged |
| 90 | Closeout call: final metrics, value assessment, and conversion discussion |

---

## Data Handling

- Parcel coordinates and the Partner's classifications are used solely to operate the pilot
  and compute the agreed metrics.
- Heavi will not publish the Partner's parcel list, pipeline, or identity without written
  consent. Aggregate, anonymized precision results may inform Heavi's product development.
- The Partner retains all rights to its parcel data and pipeline information.

---

## Conversion Discussion

At day 90, both parties review the measured precision, coverage, and time-savings value, and
discuss an **annual subscription** for continued scoring access:

- **Annual subscription:** **$25,000–$50,000 / year** (unlimited scoring), scoped to the
  Partner's expected volume and modules (solar today; hazard and trade-area as they ship).
- No obligation: a pilot that does not meet the success criteria carries no commitment, and
  the failure-reason log is delivered either way as a standalone deliverable.
